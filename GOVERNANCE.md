# Governance

Model Modding begins with lightweight governance designed to evolve with the community.

## Principles

- Decisions should be visible and documented.
- Authority should follow demonstrated contribution and stewardship.
- No single maintainer should become a permanent delivery bottleneck.
- Technical quality, safety and community health take priority over speed.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who participates through issues, discussions, reviews, documentation, evaluations, mods or code.

### Maintainers

Trusted contributors who review changes, manage releases and steward defined repository areas.

### Working-group leads

Maintainers who coordinate a domain such as evaluation, education, tooling or responsible modding.

### Founder

The founder protects the initial mission, appoints the first maintainers and resolves deadlocks until a broader maintainer council is established.

## Decision process

Routine changes are decided through pull-request review. Significant changes to the specification, governance, security model or compatibility rules should use a Model Modding Proposal (MMP).

An MMP should describe the problem, proposal, alternatives, impact, migration needs and decision record.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are selected based on sustained, constructive contributions; sound judgement; respectful collaboration; and willingness to review and support others.

## Conflicts of interest

Maintainers should disclose relevant commercial or organisational interests when participating in decisions that could materially benefit them.

## Evolution

This document is intentionally simple. Governance changes should be proposed publicly and adopted through documented consensus.
