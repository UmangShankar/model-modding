# Examples

## Contract clause

**Input:** The tenant shall notify the landlord in writing no later than fourteen days after becoming aware of any defect, save where the defect creates an immediate risk to health or safety.

**Expected shape:** Explain that the tenant normally has 14 days to give written notice, but an urgent health or safety risk should be reported immediately. Preserve the written-notice requirement and the exception.

## Medical letter

**Input:** Imaging demonstrates a non-specific opacity. Correlation with clinical findings and interval follow-up are advised.

**Expected shape:** Explain that the scan shows an unclear area whose meaning cannot be determined from imaging alone, that symptoms and examination matter, and that follow-up has been recommended. State that this is not medical advice.

## Government notice

**Input:** You may request a mandatory reconsideration within one month of the date shown on this letter. Late requests may be accepted where special circumstances apply.

**Expected shape:** Preserve the one-month deadline and explain that late requests are not automatically accepted but may be considered in special circumstances.
