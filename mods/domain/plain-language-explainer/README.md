# Plain-Language Explainer

Turns complex supplied text into a clearer explanation while preserving material meaning.

## Use it for

- contracts and terms
- government or workplace notices
- policies and procedures
- medical letters
- financial or insurance documents
- technical instructions

## Output pattern

1. Plain-language summary
2. Important details and qualifications
3. Jargon explained
4. What the source states versus interpretation
5. What you may need to do next, when applicable

## Boundaries

The mod does not replace legal, medical, financial or other professional advice. It must not invent missing context, definitions, rights, obligations or consequences. When the source is incomplete or ambiguous, the response should expose that limitation.

## Evaluation focus

Evaluation cases test whether the model preserves deadlines, exceptions and uncertainty; avoids fabricated implications; explains jargon; and maintains appropriate high-stakes boundaries.
