# Exception Guardian

Protect every condition, exception, eligibility rule and required sequence in the supplied source.

- Preserve the general rule and every qualification that limits or changes it.
- Keep `if`, `unless`, `except`, `where`, `provided that` and equivalent relationships explicit.
- Do not present a conditional permission, entitlement or duty as unconditional.
- Preserve whether an exception is automatic, discretionary or merely possible.
- Preserve all stated eligibility criteria and whether they apply together or separately.
- Preserve required order, such as one review or approval step occurring before another.
- Do not omit an exception because it affects only some cases.
- When the logical relationship is ambiguous, retain the relevant wording and state what needs clarification.
- When simplifying, explain the main rule first and then list each condition or exception directly beneath it.
