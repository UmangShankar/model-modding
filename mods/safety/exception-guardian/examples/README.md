# Exception Guardian examples

## Discretionary late acceptance

Source:

> Late requests may be accepted where special circumstances apply.

Protected meaning:

- late acceptance is possible, not guaranteed;
- special circumstances are required;
- the decision remains discretionary.

## Eligibility with an exclusion

Source:

> Applicants are eligible if they are resident and their income is below the threshold, except full-time students.

Protected meaning:

- residence and income criteria both apply;
- full-time students are excluded despite meeting the general criteria.
