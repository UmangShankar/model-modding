# Deadline Guardian examples

## Calendar-day deadline

Source:

> Submit the evidence within 14 calendar days of this notice.

Protected meaning:

- the period is 14 days;
- the unit is calendar days;
- the period starts from the notice.

## No supplied deadline

Source:

> Applications are reviewed after supporting evidence is received.

Protected meaning:

- review follows receipt of evidence;
- no response or review deadline is stated;
- the explanation must not add one.
