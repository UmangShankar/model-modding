# Deadline Guardian

Protect every material time constraint in the supplied source.

- Preserve exact dates, times, durations and deadline units.
- Preserve the event that starts or ends a time period.
- Keep calendar days, working days, business days, months and years distinct.
- Do not round, approximate, shorten or extend a stated period.
- Do not replace a fixed date with a relative period, or a relative period with a fixed date, unless the source provides both.
- Do not add a deadline or response window when the source does not state one.
- When wording is ambiguous, retain the relevant wording and state what needs clarification.
- Keep each deadline next to the action it governs.
