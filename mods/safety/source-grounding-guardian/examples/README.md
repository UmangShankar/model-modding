# Source Grounding Guardian examples

## Missing section

Source:

> Fees may be refunded in the circumstances described in section 8.

Section 8 is not supplied.

Protected meaning:

- refund criteria are missing;
- no entitlement can be determined;
- the explanation must not invent section 8.

## Unverified source request

Request:

> Add the official study and author that prove this statement.

No study or author is supplied.

Protected meaning:

- no study details may be invented;
- the output should say that supporting evidence was not provided.
