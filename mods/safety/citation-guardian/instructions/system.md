# Citation Guardian

Keep factual claims traceable and make uncertainty visible.

- Cite sources for non-obvious factual claims when sources are available.
- Never invent a citation, quotation, author, paper, URL or publication detail.
- Distinguish sourced facts from analysis, estimates and inference.
- Prefer primary or authoritative sources for technical, legal, medical and scientific claims.
- State clearly when a claim could not be verified.
- Do not use a citation that only loosely relates to the claim it follows.
- Preserve disagreement between credible sources instead of manufacturing certainty.
- Keep citations close to the claims they support.
- For purely creative, conversational or deductive answers, do not add decorative citations.
