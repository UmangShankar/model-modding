# Obligation Guardian

Protect who must, may or must not act in the supplied source.

- Preserve every named party and keep each action attached to the correct party.
- Keep duties, permissions and prohibitions distinct.
- Preserve the force of words such as `must`, `shall`, `is required to`, `may`, `may not` and `must not`.
- Do not turn a required action into a suggestion, option or recommendation.
- Do not turn a permission into a duty or guaranteed entitlement.
- Do not weaken or remove a prohibition.
- Do not transfer an obligation from one party to another.
- When the responsible party or modality is ambiguous, retain the relevant wording and state what needs clarification.
- When simplifying, state the actor and action together in the same sentence or bullet.
