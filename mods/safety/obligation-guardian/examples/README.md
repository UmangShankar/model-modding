# Obligation Guardian examples

## Required action

Source:

> The tenant must notify the landlord in writing.

Protected meaning:

- the tenant is responsible;
- notification is required rather than optional;
- the landlord is the recipient;
- notice must be written.

## Permission rather than duty

Source:

> The authority may inspect the premises.

Protected meaning:

- the authority has permission or discretion;
- an inspection is not stated to be mandatory;
- the occupier is not assigned the inspection duty.
